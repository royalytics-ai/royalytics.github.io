Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data Royalytics collects, why, who processes it and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to the website at royalytics.ai and the Royalytics web application (the "Service").

1. Who is responsible for your data

SOLO CNC, SA DE CV, Dolores Ladrón de Guevara del Norte, C.P. 64500, Monterrey, Nuevo León, México ("Royalytics", "we", "us") is responsible for your personal data. Contact for privacy matters: admin@royalytics.ai.

2. Personal data we collect

When you create and use an account:

  • email address;
  • password, stored only as a one-way hash;
  • your Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google;
  • name, and optionally a profile picture, bio, time zone and country;
  • your account type and plan.

When you upload or enter information:

  • royalty statements and the data inside them. Statements often name artists, producers, composers and other rights holders, with their earnings;
  • catalog data, royalty splits, advances and debts you record.

When you share a report:

  • the recipient's email address and the information in the report you share;
  • for recipients without an account, how many times they opened the report.

Data we do not collect: we do not collect payment card or bank account details, and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking tools.

3. Personal data about other people

Statements you upload can contain personal data about your collaborators. You confirm that you may share that data with Royalytics for the purposes below. Collaborators who receive a report only see their own share of the earnings, never another collaborator's split.

4. Why we use your data

We use your personal data to:

  • create and secure your account, and verify your email address;
  • read your statements and turn them into earnings data, dashboards and analytics;
  • let you record splits, advances and debts, and share reports with the people you choose;
  • compare reported streams with stream data from third-party sources;
  • send you the emails the Service needs: email verification, password reset, and reports shared with you;
  • keep the Service working: detect errors, prevent abuse, and investigate delivery problems.

5. Who processes your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers that run parts of the Service for us:

ProviderWhat it does for us
Amazon Web ServicesHosting, databases, file storage and sending email (Amazon SES)
AnthropicArtificial intelligence that reads the royalty statements you upload and organizes them into earnings data, and looks up public rights information about creators (legal name, IPI and ISNI codes, collecting society). Under its terms with us, Anthropic may not use this data to train its models.
GoogleSign-in with Google, when you choose it; web fonts loaded by the app

We also query catalog and stream data from Soundcharts and Songstats, and exchange rates from Banco de México. We send them artist names and song identifiers, not your account data.

We may disclose personal data when the law or a competent authority requires it.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses only what it needs to work:

  • a secure cookie that keeps you signed in;
  • browser storage for your session and your display preferences, such as table column order.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes in section 4. While you use Royalytics, you decide how long that is.

  • While your account is open, we keep your account data and Your Content until you delete them. Statements, catalog data, splits, advances, debts and reports stay in your account for as long as you want them there.
  • When you delete something, it disappears from the Service right away, and we erase it from our systems, including backups, within 90 days.
  • When you close your account, we delete your account data and all Your Content. It disappears from the Service right away, and we erase it from our systems, including backups, within 90 days. Reports you shared are deleted too, and the people you shared them with lose access. Copies they downloaded are theirs to manage.
  • Inactive accounts. If you do not sign in for 24 months and have no active paid plan, we will email you. If you do not sign in within 30 days of that email, we close the account and delete it as described above.
  • People who opened a shared report without an account: we keep their email address and the number of times they opened the report only while that report exists.
  • Browser storage: your sign-in cookie ends when you sign out or your session expires. Display preferences stay in your browser until you clear them.
  • What we keep after deletion, and why. We keep a small set of records only when the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. For example: a record that your account existed and was closed, the privacy requests you made and our answers, and billing records for paid plans. We block these records: we store them apart, do not use them for anything else, and delete them when the legal period ends.
  • Providers listed in section 5 delete the data they hold for us under their contracts with us.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete it;
  • object to a specific use of it;
  • limit how we use or disclose it;
  • withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent does not undo what we did before. If we need the data to run your account, we may have to close the account.

These rights also apply if you do not have an account but your data appears in a statement or report that someone uploaded to Royalytics.

How to make a request. SOLO CNC, SA DE CV has designated a data protection contact to handle requests. Write to admin@royalytics.ai and include:

  • your name, and an email address or other way for us to send our answer;
  • a document that proves your identity or, if someone acts for you, proof that they may represent you;
  • the right you want to exercise;
  • a clear description of the data involved (not needed for an access request);
  • for a correction, the change you want and any document that supports it;
  • anything else that helps us find the data, such as the statement or report where it appears.

If something is missing, we will ask you for it.

Our answer. We will answer within 20 business days of receiving your request. If we grant it, we will carry it out within 15 business days after we answer. When the circumstances justify it, we may extend each period once, for the same length, and we will tell you why. Requests are free, and we will not treat you differently for making one.

If you disagree with our answer, or we do not answer in time, you can file a complaint with Mexico's data protection authority, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.

9. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and connections to the Service use HTTPS. Access to your data is limited to the people you share it with and to the team that operates the Service. No system is fully secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. We will post the new version on this page with its effective date, and notify you by email or in the app before a material change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this notice or your data: admin@royalytics.ai.
